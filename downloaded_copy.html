The Butterfly Wonder: Unwrapping the Wonder of the New Identity in Christ

To come to Christ without fully understanding the revolutionary transformation that takes place within the spirit of man is missing out on what all Jesus did in order to redeem man. There is an underlying message in the New Testament that every Christian must fully understand in order to walk into the NEW CREATURE that God has given us when born again. The New Testament talks about the ‘old man’ and the ‘new man’, or the ‘old self’ or the ‘new self’. The scriptures say that the old man is corrupt, in every area, spiritually, morally, and physically because the old self is the offspring of Satan. It is of the seed of the deception of Satan. Consequently, God told the serpent in Genesis 3:15 that his seed would then be corrupt, and therefore be the enemy of the seed of the women… “I will make your offspring and her offspring enemies of each other” (Genesis 3:15a).
