What It Means to Be Born Again

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi there,{{/if}}

Too many believers have been saved, yet they still live as though nothing fundamentally changed.

They’re all holy and sanctified and purified when coming out of the Sunday service, but the rest of the week they do not know how to apply the principles of the Kingdom so that they live as CONQUERORS.

They keep trying to repair the old person, overcome the old identity, earn God’s acceptance, and prove that they are finally good enough. They judge God’s Word by their experience, remain more conscious of failure than righteousness, and call this struggle normal Christianity.

But being born again is not self-improvement.

It is not a renovation of the old life. It is the birth of an entirely new creation.

At the cross, Jesus did more than forgive your past and secure your place in heaven. He entered your fallen condition so that you could enter His resurrected life.

He became what you were so that you could become what He is.

He was made sin so you could be made righteous.

He became weak so you could become strong.

He was condemned so you could be justified.

He died so you could live.

The old you was not repaired. The old you was crucified.

When you received Christ, your spirit was recreated by the Spirit of God. Your citizenship changed. You were transferred from darkness into light, welcomed into God’s family, and made an heir with Christ.

Yet many believers continue returning to the old identity, like someone receiving a brand-new car but climbing back into the broken one out of habit.

This class will reveal what actually happened when you were born again. You will discover why you are no longer defined by sin, fear, trauma, guilt, or spiritual inferiority. You will learn how to renew your mind, speak in agreement with God’s Word, live from Christ’s finished work, and exercise the authority that belongs to you as a child of the King.

Stop trying to become what God says you already are.

You are not merely an old sinner struggling to improve.

You are a new creation. Now it is time to live like one.
