You Rule! As One Having Authority

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi there,{{/if}}

Stop begging a distant God for scraps when you hold the King’s scepter.

Most Christians live in a state of spiritual “identity theft” — trapped in a survival mindset, begging God to help them survive another week, and treating the Bible like a textbook sitting idle on a nightstand. We carry an unsheathed sword, while staying silent in the face of sickness, lack, and demonic chaos.

In the spiritual realm, silence isn’t peaceful — silence is surrender.

Asking God to do something He already paid for at the Cross only affirms your lack and keeps you bound like a slave.

Here is the legal reality of the universe: Jesus performed a total takeover at the Resurrection, stripping Satan of his stolen title deed and grabbing ALL authority in Heaven and on Earth!

Jesus wasn’t the exception; He was the blueprint. At the Cross, the ultimate exchange took place: Christ took your death so you could take His throne, giving you His Name, His Holy Spirit, and the legal power of attorney to govern Earth in His stead.

Every promise in Christ is already an unchangeable, resounding “YES”!

Enforce Your Kingdom Dominion

Move from a wish to a decree.
• Ditch the survival mindset: Stop talking to God about how big the problem is — start speaking to the problem with authority about how big your God is!
• Unsheathe the Sword of the Spirit: Your tongue is the handle of the Word. Speak, command, and release spiritual artillery out loud to drive darkness out.
• Reign as a ruler in life: Visualize the victory, command physical facts to align with spiritual Truth, and start praising God for the harvest the moment you speak.

You are not a spiritual beggar asking for a miracle; you are a Spirit-filled delivery system sent to execute judgment and destroy the works of the devil.
