Jesus Boot Camp Class 7

Dare to Be Different

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi there,{{/if}}

Too many believers were created to rule, yet they live imprisoned by fear, small thinking, and the opinions of the world.

They allow circumstances to define what is possible. They rehearse problems until those problems become larger than God’s promises. They carry dreams in their hearts but never pursue them because failure, criticism, and uncertainty keep whispering, “Stay where it is safe.”

But you were never called to live a safe, ordinary, counterfeit life.

You were created to live courageously, exercise Christ’s authority, and fulfill a destiny that reaches beyond yourself.

Through His death and resurrection, Jesus did more than forgive sin. He reclaimed the authority Adam lost and then commissioned His followers to administer that authority on earth. You are not a powerless spectator waiting for heaven to act.

You are Christ’s chosen, deputized representative.

Yet authority will accomplish nothing when your mind remains ruled by fear and unbelief. Whatever you continually focus on grows within you. Fear can become a master, but so can faith. Your thoughts, words, imagination, and daily obedience are seeds that will eventually produce a harvest.

This class will challenge you to break agreement with the world’s limitations and allow God’s Word to reshape your vision. You will discover how to take thoughts captive, speak life instead of defeat, meditate on God’s promises, and keep pursuing His purpose when circumstances appear impossible.

You will learn from Joseph that vision survives ridicule, betrayal, delay, and hardship when the heart remains faithful. You will also discover why a godly dream must become more than a wish. It must produce goals, discipline, service, endurance, and action.

Stop rehearsing what could go wrong.

Stop allowing fear to bury the destiny God placed within you. When criticism, exposure, or uncertainty knocks on the door, send faith to answer.

God did not create you to blend into a fearful world.

Dare to think differently. Dare to believe beyond what you see. Dare to live out God’s destiny.
