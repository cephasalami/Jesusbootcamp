Jesus Boot Camp Class 8

The Power of God’s Word

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi there,{{/if}}

Too many believers are starving while sitting beside an unlimited supply of spiritual food.

They ask God for strength, direction, faith, and victory, yet allow days to pass without opening the very Word that provides them. Then, when fear, temptation, doubt, or discouragement arrives, they discover that their spirit has nothing stored up with which to fight.

But God’s Word is not ordinary information.

It is living seed, spiritual nourishment, divine light, and a weapon placed in your hands.

Jesus explained that everything in the Kingdom begins with the seed of the Word. When that seed enters a receptive heart, takes root, and is protected from distraction, worry, persecution, and worldly desire, it produces a harvest far greater than what was planted.

Your entire spiritual life is shaped by what you do with that seed.

The Word you merely hear may inform you. The Word you receive, meditate upon, and obey will transform you.

This class will reveal why God’s Word must become more necessary to you than daily food. You will discover how Scripture renews your mind, reveals God’s will, strengthens faith, cleanses your heart, guides your decisions, and produces the character of Christ within you.

You will also learn why the enemy works so aggressively to interrupt your time in the Word. He knows that when truth fills your mind, there is less room for his darkness. When doubt attacks, the Word becomes your cure. When temptation comes, it becomes your sword. When your path feels uncertain, it becomes the lamp beneath your feet.

Jesus defeated Satan by declaring, “It is written.”

The same weapon is now available to you.

Stop trying to win spiritual battles with an empty heart and an unused sword.

Do not merely admire the Bible. Plant it. Eat it. Meditate upon it. Speak it. Obey it. Let it read you, correct you, prune you, and rebuild your life from the inside out.

The strength you need is not hidden from you.

It is waiting in the Word of God.
