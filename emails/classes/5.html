Jesus Boot Camp Class 5

God’s Gift to Believers, the Holy Spirit

{{#if first_name}}Hi {{first_name}},{{else}}Hi there,{{/if}}

Too many believers are trying to fulfill a supernatural calling through natural strength.

They love God, attend church, read Scripture, and sincerely want to make a difference. Yet they remain exhausted, intimidated, and frustrated because they are attempting to produce through human effort what only the Holy Spirit can accomplish.

But God never intended you to follow Jesus alone.

The Holy Spirit is not an abstract force, a religious emotion, or an optional experience for a select few. He is God’s personal presence living and working within you.

Jesus even told His disciples that it was better for Him to leave so the Holy Spirit could come. Why? Because the Spirit who empowered Jesus would no longer rest upon only one Man.

He would come to dwell within EVERY believer.

The same Spirit who brought order out of chaos, raised Jesus from the dead, transformed fearful disciples into bold witnesses, and empowered the early Church now lives in YOU.

He was not sent merely to comfort you.

He was sent to transform you, guide you, and empower you to demonstrate the Kingdom of God.

This next class will reveal why the Christian life is not sustained by rules, talent, willpower, or self-help. You will discover how the Holy Spirit produces love, joy, peace, courage, obedience, and spiritual fruit that you could never manufacture on your own.

You will also learn what it means to receive dunamis power, the supernatural ability to become a living witness through bold proclamation, healing, deliverance, signs, and visible demonstrations of God’s Kingdom.

A powerful phone is useless when it has no charge. A flashlight cannot shine without batteries. In the same way, you cannot fulfill your divine mission while ignoring the power source God has already provided.

Stop serving God through exhausted human effort.

Stop trying to manufacture fruit, overcome temptation, discern direction, or fulfill the Great Commission by yourself.

The Father has given you His greatest gift.

It is time to know the Holy Spirit, depend upon Him, and allow His power to flow through your life.
