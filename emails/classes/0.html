Christ called us to transform the world, not settle for a lukewarm, Sunday-only faith. Today, too many believers sit in neutral, knowing the Word intellectually but lacking the practical revelation to live it out.

The Jesus Boot Camp is the missing bridge between Christian knowledge and active commission.

Over 90 days, this intensive discipleship journey systematically activates your faith through three core pillars:

Identity — Knowing who you are in Christ.

Authority — Understanding the spiritual power you carry.

Commission — Stepping out as an activated ambassador of the Kingdom.

Go beyond passive attendance. Enter the framework designed to ignite the Body of Christ from the inside out.
