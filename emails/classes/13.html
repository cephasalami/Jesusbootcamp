You cannot be a “little bit” saved.
Dive Into Class 13: The True Gospel →

Just like a girl can’t be a little bit pregnant or a little bit married, you cannot be a part-time Christian.

Yet millions of believers treat their faith like a weekly social club — relegating the King of the Universe to a one-hour box on Sunday mornings while remaining trapped in the same old cycles of guilt, fear, and frustration Monday through Saturday.

Trying to regulate a fallen nature with rules, rituals, and surface-level denominational theology doesn’t work. You cannot clean up or reverse a spiritual contamination; it requires a complete system reboot.

Enter the Cosmic Game-Changer: Jesus didn’t come to start a religion, sell a philosophy, or make bad people “good”.

He came to execute a total spiritual metamorphosis. Through His death and resurrection, He completely wiped out the corrupted, virus-ridden “junk-code” of your old sin-nature. Your spirit was dead and separated from God, but the moment you stepped “into Christ,” your old self was forever nailed to the cross. You aren’t just forgiven — you are a brand-new creation.

Look at the Divine Exchange: He took everything you were so you could step into everything He is.

He became sin so you could become the absolute righteousness of God.

He took on shame and disfigurement so you could walk in unfiltered glory.

His body was beaten and broken so that physical, emotional, and spiritual healing would legally belong to you right now.

He was cast out of the Father’s presence so you could stand in full fellowship and sit with Him in heavenly places.

Salvation is free to you, but it cost the precious blood of the Son of God.

Treating that sacrifice like a dry doctrine is an insult to the price He paid.

Stop settling for a powerless, pie-in-the-sky faith. Open this lesson today, lay hold of your legal covenant signed in blood, and start walking in the bold, unstoppable authority that has already been credited to your account.
