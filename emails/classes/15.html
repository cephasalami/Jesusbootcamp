“Well, it is what it is.”
Access Class 15: How Faith Works →

How many times have you looked at a bad diagnosis, a dry bank account, or a mountain of circumstances and thrown up your hands with that defeated phrase? We treat faith like an agonizing exercise in perspiration — straining, sweating, and trying to “feel spiritual” enough to convince God to move.

Worse, we pray on Sunday and then dig up our own seeds with doubt and fear on Monday afternoon.

Faith doesn’t respond to your tears, your desperation, or your begging. And in the Kingdom of God, we NEVER settle for “it is what it is”.

Here is the dramatic shift: Faith is not a mystery, a feeling, or a leap in the dark. It is an unchanging spiritual law.

Faith isn’t about having confidence in your own belief — it’s simply knowing that God cannot lie and cannot fail.

What appears in the physical realm is not the final word. You don’t have to wait for God to move; the Cross was a total restoration of spiritual authority back to YOU, and Jesus has already finished the work.

Activate your Kingdom Currency: You are not a beggar begging Heaven for help; you are a son equipped with authority to command God’s will on Earth.

Enforce the spiritual law of faith: A heart full of faith that refuses to speak is an engine idling in neutral. Your mouth is the governing tool of your destiny.

Speak higher reality: When you declare His Word over your body, finances, and family, your words carry the atomic power to make the unseen manifest into the seen.

Stop double-minded wavering: Hold fast to your confession without digging up the seed through doubt.

Stop letting unbelief limit what God can do in your life. It’s time to enforce Heaven’s law of faith and step into what is already yours.

Dive into today’s session, learn the exact mechanics of how faith works, and start commanding your mountains to move!
