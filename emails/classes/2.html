Too many believers know they are forgiven, but they have never understood what they were restored to.

They still approach life feeling powerless, spiritually inferior, and dependent on God to do everything for them. They pray for the authority they have already received, wait for the power that already lives within them, and tolerate situations Jesus commanded His followers to confront.

But salvation did more than rescue you from sin.

It restored your identity, your purpose, and your authority.

Man was originally created in the image of God to exercise dominion, represent His Kingdom, and carry His presence into the earth. The fall fractured that calling, but through the cross, Jesus accomplished the greatest exchange in history. He took YOUR sin so that you could become the righteousness of God in Him.

You are not merely a forgiven version of your former self.

You are a completely new creation, filled with divine life and authorized to represent Christ.

The same Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead now lives in you. You are complete in Him. You share in His authority. You have been entrusted with the ministry of reconciliation and commissioned to push back darkness through truth, compassion, healing, and bold obedience.

Yet knowing this is not enough.

A Ferrari sitting unused in a garage may be powerful, but it accomplishes nothing until someone turns the key. In the same way, your authority in Christ will remain theological information until you begin exercising it.

This class will challenge you to move from legal possession to practical experience. You will discover how to renew your mind, speak to mountains, act on compassion, confront sickness and oppression, and live every day as Heaven’s ambassador.

Stop asking God to give you what Christ has already placed within you.

You are not waiting to become powerful. You are learning to use the power you already have.

The world does not need more Christians who merely know the truth.

It needs believers who know who they are and boldly live it.
